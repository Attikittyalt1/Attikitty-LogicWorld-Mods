# Attikitty's Selection Tools
Adds a selection clipboard & history for greater selection management.

Relevant keybinds can be found at the Controls/Attikitty's Selection Tools tab of your settings menu.

Current keybinds added by this mod:
- `Undo Selection`: Undos the latest change to your current selection.
- `Redo Selection`: Redos the latest change to your current selection.
- `Clear Selection History`: Clears your selection history.
- `Store Alternate Selection`: Sets your selection clipboard to the current selection.
- `Load Alternate Selection`: Sets your current selection to the selection clipboard.
- `Clear Alternate Selection`: Clears the clipboard.
- `Swap Alternate Selection`: Swaps your current selection with the selection clipboard.
- `Add Alternate Selection`: Adds your current selection to the selection clipboard without overwriting it.
- `Remove Alternate Selection`: Removes your current selection from the selection clipboard without overwriting it.

Max selection history length can be adjusted in the Gameplay/Attikitty's Selection Tools tab of your settings menu.

## Install / Dependencies:

Just drop the `AttikittySelectionTools` folder into your `GameData` folder.

For this mod to function, you will need two additional mods: `EccsLogicWorldAPI` & `HarmonyForLogicWorld`
You can find them in [this mod collection](https://github.com/Ecconia/Ecconia-LogicWorld-Mods/tree/master) by @Ecconia.
